# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

**Employers page**: HR Directors, Benefits Managers, CFOs, and Benefits Brokers at mid-market employers (50–500 employees). Context: researching GLP-1 benefit management solutions, often post-renewal season. Job: evaluate whether NexaCare solves a cost problem they already know they have, and get enough confidence to book a call.

**Portal**: Clinic coordinators and practice managers at independent GLP-1 clinics. Context: mid-workflow, checking on their enrolled patients. Job: monitor patient retention, enroll new patients, catch escalations fast.

**Admin**: NexaCare founders only. Job: onboard new clinics quickly and correctly.

## Product Purpose

NexaCare is the operational infrastructure layer for GLP-1 clinics and employer benefit programs. CarePath is the 90-day SMS engagement program that keeps patients on their medication. The website exists to generate inbound leads from employers and build trust for clinic coordinators who are evaluating the platform.

## Brand Personality

Clinically precise, operationally serious, quietly confident. Not warm-and-approachable consumer health. Not cold enterprise B2B either. Sits in the register of "a smart operator who knows this space cold and has the data to back it up."

Three-word version: **credible, focused, operational**.

Emotional goal for employer visitors: "These people understand the problem I have better than I do."

## Anti-references

- Avoid: white + teal healthcare branding (Hims/Hers, Noom, consumer wellness apps)
- Avoid: glassmorphism-heavy SaaS dashboards (generic dark-mode startup aesthetic)
- Avoid: stock photography of smiling patients or doctors in white coats
- Avoid: feel-good language ("empowering patients on their journey") — this is an ops-and-ROI conversation
- Avoid: hero-metric grids with big numbers and gradient accents (explicit impeccable ban — already partially violated in current /employers)

## Design Principles

1. **Data earns trust, not design.** Every visual element must serve the data, argument, or workflow — never the other way around.
2. **Speed signals professionalism.** Clinic coordinators are busy. The portal should feel faster to use than it looks.
3. **Typography does the heavy lifting.** NexaCare's credibility lives in the quality of its copy and the legibility of its typography, not in decorative chrome.
4. **Dark is a posture, not a default.** The dark navy palette communicates precision and seriousness. It should feel more like a Bloomberg terminal than a consumer app.
5. **B2B restraint.** This is a productivity and compliance tool for professionals. Every interaction should feel efficient, not delightful. Reserve personality for moments of real consequence (enrollment confirmation, retention milestone).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target: WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- Enrollment forms available in EN, ES, FR — the portal must handle multilingual patient names without breaking layout
- Reduced-motion must be respected (no exceptions — clinic staff with vestibular disorders use this daily)
- Touch targets minimum 44x44px (clinic staff may use tablets at point of care)
